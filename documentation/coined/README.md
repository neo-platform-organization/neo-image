# Coined terms

Terms proposed during neo's design discussions. These describe design aims;
they do not establish novelty or completed implementation. They are not terms
attributed to Kulisz or C2.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [Interpretive neutrality](interpretive-neutrality.md) | Authoring form does not permanently determine how a program may understand the shared graph. |
| [Generative coherence](generative-coherence.md) | A few consistent organizing ideas enable many useful capabilities. |

See the [language contract](../language-v1.md) for implemented guarantees and
remaining requirements.
